# Mustafar cloth and lava assets

Created with the built-in image-generation tool, followed by PNG export, uniform garment fitting and lava border wrap correction.

## Export specifications

| File | Canvas | Alpha | Ground anchor (top-left origin) |
|---|---|---|---|
| robe_obiwan.png | 128 × 96 | Real RGBA | (64, 52) |
| cloak_anakin.png | 144 × 104 | Real RGBA | (70, 55) |
| lava_tile.png | 512 × 512 | Opaque RGB | Not applicable |

Garment opaque silhouettes fit inside the projected guide volumes; original guide pixels are absent. Soft contact shadows use fractional alpha. Clothes are irregular heaps and therefore do not fill every corner of the footprint guides. Lava wraps along both axes; opposite outer edge pixels match exactly. The tile was visually checked in a 2 × 2 repeat.

## Reference evidence

- Viewed film still for lava: https://www.starwars.com/databank/mustafar (official still: mustafar-2-retina_6e99980e.jpeg).
- Viewed film close-up for Anakin's hood and coarse woven fabric: https://www.inverse.com/entertainment/star-wars-revenge-of-the-sith-anakin-obi-wan-alternate-scene . The requested black color is retained; the still's lit fabric appears warmer.
- The exact discarded-robe fold layouts are inferred, not copied from an observed film frame. Obi-Wan's brown color follows the supplied brief.
- Attempted interactive material reference: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/lava-7b9171dd1613444985ae03db3fa63f9c . The viewer reported a render failure, so no claim of rotating or inspecting the loaded 3D model is made. No model geometry or textures were downloaded or reused.
- Existing conf_floor.png was used as the restrained baked-style reference; it was not used as clothing material.
