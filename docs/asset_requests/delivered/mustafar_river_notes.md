## Observed references and adaptations

Inspected final-film frames from the jump/duel sequence in Todd Vaziri's published effects breakdown. The wider final frame shows the square skiff and its thin rear control post; close frames do not expose the droid's whole mechanism. Also inspected a published DLC-13 illustration hosted by RPGGamer: broad shallow round cap, paired sensor eyes, exposed stalk, jointed mining arms and collection bucket. For the game's shallow guide, the droid is deliberately compact, with folded manipulators and the bucket absent. The illustration's blue eyes were changed to amber as requested. This is a guide-fitted gameplay adaptation rather than an exact reconstruction of every film detail.

Licensed toy-photo listings and a matching Cults platform listing were found, but the detailed toy-image downloads were blocked and no 3D viewer was rotated in this batch. Those uninspected images are not claimed as visual evidence.
